## Privacy copy

### Your mortgage data stays with you

Mortgage Manager stores the mortgage figures you enter in your web browser on your device. It does not require an account or upload those figures to a Mortgage Manager cloud service.

When you choose Online mode, the app may fetch a small dated market benchmark file. Your mortgage figures are not included in that request; the calculations are performed locally in your browser.

## What Online mode means

Online mode does **not** connect to your lender or search every mortgage product in the market.

It refreshes a small set of dated Bank of England quoted mortgage-rate benchmarks so Mortgage Manager can show an indicative future-payment scenario alongside your own mortgage projection.

These benchmarks are not personalised offers and do not include eligibility, fees or lender-specific criteria.

## FAQ

### Is this a mortgage comparison service?
No. Mortgage Manager is a planning tool. It uses benchmark market rates to help illustrate possible future payments, not to recommend or source mortgage products.

### Does it connect to my bank or lender?
No.

### Do I need an account?
No.

### Is there a subscription?
No. Offline Edition is intended as a one-off purchase.

### Where is my mortgage information stored?
In the browser storage on the Windows PC/browser profile where you use the app. You can export a backup yourself.

### What happens if I clear my browser data?
Your locally stored Mortgage Manager data can be removed. Export a backup from Setup & data before clearing site data, changing browsers or moving PC.

### Does it work without internet?
The core mortgage calculations and saved information are designed to work offline after the app has been launched and cached. Online mode is only needed to refresh market benchmark data.

### Are the market rates live mortgage deals?
No. They are dated Bank of England quoted household mortgage-rate benchmarks used for illustration.

### Does Mortgage Manager give financial advice?
No. It is an illustrative planning tool. Important decisions should be checked against your lender, broker or other appropriate professional information.

### What Windows versions are supported?
Offline V1 is packaged for modern Windows PCs with Windows PowerShell and a current web browser. Final release testing should specify the exact supported versions after the browser QA pass.
